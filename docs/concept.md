### Summary

This will be a fully annotated and movable map of the night sky and universe. These images can be contrasted and compared to the same view at different times to study the change. the user can set the times and click play to watch a video like playback of the image throughout time. The images can also be rendered in different wavelengths of light. these different options will allow users to see object movement and faint objects. Users will be able to pan the view around with the mouse or enter coordinates (Right Ascension and declination) to conduct the study. every known object will be annotated and clickable to where it'll give the name and a more info button that brings them to the page with the proper documentation on info of the object (when it was discovered, who discovered it, what it is, etc). The data will come from SPHEREx, and Gaia, and WISE with all of the instrumentation onboard. this tool will be fully forward facing on the web for all to use.

Create a full annotated (labeled) and movable map of the sky and universe. Will be hosted on the web and actively updated every six months following the scan time of SphereX. Allows for infinite zoom and clickable space objects. when space object is clicked, it will open a window that give info on the object, and read more brings to the database entry of the space object. The map can be changed varied on time. changes in data compared to time will be highlighted (Eg. A star pulsing, comet moving, etc) allowing for studies focused on the changing universe. you can use the mouse to select specific spots in the sky to highlight and analyze changes. the map can also filter through all the sensors onboard the SphereX

I want this program to be used for all types of space science and research. Galaxy, Pulsar, Star, Exoplanet, Universe research. Even inter solar system research on our planets and moons.

Work to fully map our solar system. all planets, dwarf planets, comets, rouge objects, everything.
